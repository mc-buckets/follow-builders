*How Autonomous Labs Will Transform Scientific Research: Ginkgo Bioworks' Jason Kelly*

Ginkgo Bioworks founder and CEO Jason Kelly explains why AI will fundamentally transform biotechnology in ways that previous tech revolutions never did. Unlike social media or internet infrastructure—which were "back office IT crap" to biotech—AI is actually disrupting how science gets done at a foundational level.

Kelly bootstrapped Ginkgo for five years (2008-2014) before raising capital because biotech VCs at the time were skeptical of young founders. The company's mission has evolved from "making biology programmable" in the traditional sense to leveraging AI to accelerate biological research and development. Kelly emphasizes that autonomous labs powered by AI will compress timelines and unlock new possibilities in biopharma that were previously impossible.

Key insight: "All of the previous revolutions in tech...have been totally meaningless to biotechnology and biopharma...this is actually gonna change the fundamentals of how we do science and our big science industries like biopharma are gonna get disrupted."

The disruption is happening faster than most expect, and companies that adapt their processes to autonomous, AI-driven workflows will gain significant competitive advantages in drug discovery, manufacturing optimization, and bioprocess development.
