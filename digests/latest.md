*Claude Blog*
<https://claude.com/blog/claude-in-chrome-generally-available|Claude in Chrome is generally available>

Claude in Chrome is now live for all paid plans and can take autonomous browser actions without manual approval for each step. A safety classifier validates every action before it runs. The release comes after extensive prompt injection hardening: with probes and automatic approval classifiers in place, no attacks succeeded against Claude Sonnet 5 or Opus 5 in testing, and just 0.3% got through against Fable 5. The feature covers internal dashboards, legacy systems, and vendor portals that don't connect directly to Claude — anything accessible via your existing logins. Enterprise admins can manage it in Organization Settings and restrict it to approved domains.
