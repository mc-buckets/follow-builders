*No Priors — "Scaling Global Organizations in the Age of AI with ServiceNow CEO Bill McDermott"*

_The Takeaway:_ Enterprise workflow platforms are not being disrupted by AI — they are becoming the irreplaceable execution layer that turns AI's recommendations into actual business outcomes.

Bill McDermott, CEO of ServiceNow, built his leadership philosophy running a Long Island deli at age 16 before a career that includes transforming SAP and growing ServiceNow into a $13B+ platform. His core argument against the "SaaSpocalypse" narrative is economic: replacing a well-integrated enterprise platform with AI-generated code costs 10 times more once you account for the human capital, GPU bills, and token costs.

The conceptual unlock he offers: "AI thinks, workflow acts." An LLM can generate a recommendation instantly, but it does not close a compensation dispute that needs to route through HR, finance, legal, and compliance. That cross-departmental workflow execution is the moat — and it won't evaporate.

On the talent side, McDermott is clear-eyed: agents already handle 90% of ServiceNow's own customer service cases, and headcount growth will slow significantly across the industry as agents absorb tactical work. What stays human is judgment, relationship management, and trust. "People make mistakes — they never will forgive software for making a mistake." That asymmetry explains why enterprise customers demand deterministic, context-aware systems over probabilistic models, however capable.

His broader point on adoption: most companies globally are still in the experiment phase, but first movers are now pushing hard into agentic business models, rethinking headcount, and pursuing implementation timelines measured in days rather than months.
