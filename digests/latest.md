*Dan Shipper* — Every CEO

Shipper posted a detailed critique of an AI-and-jobs report, taking aim at two core assumptions: that jobs can be fully decomposed into task lists, and that automation always reduces human labor. His counterpoint: automation often _creates_ multiples more work for humans. And a job isn't just a list of tasks — it's a particular way of seeing and caring about the world. As tools and circumstances change, the same underlying cares generate entirely new tasks. That process is missing from most economic models.
